All three downloaded samples below are published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
They are bundled locally; gameplay never contacts the source websites. Credits are retained voluntarily.

| File            | Creator and source                                                                                 | Preparation                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `propeller.wav` | modusmogulus — [Airplane Propeller Loop](https://freesound.org/people/modusmogulus/sounds/789390/) | Public HQ MP3 preview decoded to mono PCM, normalized, 80 ms loop seam crossfade.                                                                                   |
| `jet.wav`       | qubodup — [Jet Turbine Noise.flac](https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/205581/)            | Public HQ MP3 preview, seconds 20–28 extracted, mono PCM, normalized, 80 ms loop seam crossfade. Uploader identifies original as a US government engine-test video. |
| `gunfire.wav`   | iamoneabe — [Random gunfire SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/random-gunfire-sfx)               | Original WAV, trimmed to onset and 200 ms tail, mono PCM, normalized and tail faded.                                                                                |

These are generic sound-design sources, not authenticated recordings of the modeled aircraft or its cannon. Cannon playback is shortened and pitch-adjusted for each aircraft. Preview-derived engine samples are lossy sources even though the prepared files use PCM.

Wind, afterburner reinforcement, gear motion, and touchdown are generated by `src/audio/audio.js` using filtered noise, motor tones, and volume envelopes. Engine, wind, and boost levels dip briefly during gunfire or gear movement to keep these effects audible. The generated engine fallback is used if an asset fails to load.

The sonic-boom cue is original procedural audio from `src/audio/sonicBoom.js`: two short
pressure-like cracks and a fading low rumble, synthesized once when audio unlocks.
It uses no downloaded recording or additional license. It shares the normal
compressor, volume, mute and cockpit filter; engine/wind/boost dip briefly so the
boom stays audible. Pausing, leaving the page or restarting stops an active cue.

## Listening checklist

Start a flight and tap/click or press a control if audio has not started. Browsers require a user gesture. Default volume is 55%; Settings → Sound controls mute and volume, saved on this device.

| Sound / behavior   | How to trigger                                                               | Expected result                                                                                     |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Propeller          | Choose Saint-Cyr; increase/decrease thrust with W/S or mobile slider         | Continuous engine loop; power changes pitch and volume smoothly. Idle remains audible at 0%.        |
| Jet                | Choose Luxeuil; change thrust                                                | Turbine loop grows with engine power, with smooth spool response.                                   |
| Afterburner        | Mirage: hold W at full thrust; mobile: hold slider beyond 100% in BOOST area | Extra low roar; release removes it while normal engine remains.                                     |
| Wind               | Accelerate in either aircraft                                                | Airflow grows with airspeed, even if thrust is reduced.                                             |
| Gun / cannon       | Hold Space or mobile FIRE                                                    | Light aircraft fires slower; Mirage fires rapid bursts. Release stops new shots, with a short tail. |
| Gear motor         | Mirage: press G or mobile Gear button in flight                              | Mechanical noise only while gear is moving; stops at either end.                                    |
| Touchdown          | Land successfully                                                            | Brief thump, stronger for harder landings. No repeated thumps while taxiing.                        |
| Sonic boom | Mirage: take off, retract gear and accelerate upward through Mach 1 (1,235 km/h in this game), normally using afterburner | One short double-crack boom with a rumble. No loop while supersonic. To repeat, wait at least six simulation seconds, slow below Mach 0.96, then accelerate through Mach 1 again. |
| Cockpit filtering  | Switch between Chase and Cockpit                                             | Cockpit is quieter and muffles exterior high frequencies.                                           |
| Pause / background | Open Settings with P/gear; switch tab or app                                 | Audio fades out on pause and silences in background; resumes with flight.                           |
| Mute / volume      | Settings → Sound, then return to flight                                      | Chosen level applies and survives reload.                                                           |

Native iPhone Safari/Home Screen listening should be checked on a real device; browser automation does not prove device audio routing or perceived sound quality.

The Mach cue deliberately gives the player audible feedback at the aircraft's
transition. It is not a model of a ground observer hearing a passing shock wave.
There is no delayed playback if the crossing happened while muted or before audio
was unlocked. The accompanying brief vapor cone is a stylized cue; actual visible
condensation depends on atmospheric conditions.
