# Air force badge artwork

`french-air-force-original.svg` preserves the original French Air and Space Force logo from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Logo_de_l%27Arm%C3%A9e_de_l%27Air_et_de_l%27Espace.svg). Author: Armée de l’Air et de l’Espace; vectorisation: Dorian crd. Commons marks it public domain under PD-textlogo and notes that official insignia may have restrictions independent of copyright.

`french-air-force.svg` is the dark-background variant used on the Luxeuil card: only the near-black (`#1a1a1a`) fills are replaced with white. Blue, red, geometry, and transparency are unchanged.

`french-air-force.png` is the earlier unmodified user-supplied reference; the card now uses the SVG.

The Tehran card reuses `../golden-crown/iiaf-emblem.svg`; see that directory’s source note.
